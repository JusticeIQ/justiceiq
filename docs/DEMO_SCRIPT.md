# Demo Presentation Script

Pairs with the interactive `/demo` page (18-step guided tour with jump links). Suggested runtime:
8–12 minutes.

1. **Landing page (`/`)** — "SolonIQ is the law-firm side of a two-product ecosystem. JusticeChamp
   helps consumers document a claim; SolonIQ is where a firm receives, triages, and manages that
   referral all the way through to a resolved matter."
2. **Login (`/login`)** — Click "Continue with demo law firm." No password needed for the demo.
3. **Dashboard (`/dashboard`)** — Point out new referrals, matters requiring attention, upcoming
   deadlines, and the case pipeline chart. "This is what a managing partner sees every morning."
4. **New Referrals (`/referrals`)** — Toggle between table and Kanban view. Filter by practice area
   or urgency to show sorting/filtering is real, not decorative.
5. **Open referral `ref-1`** — A brand-new personal injury referral from JusticeChamp. Walk through
   the claim-readiness score, structured claim summary, and the assessment section — emphasize it's
   labeled "preliminary intake analysis, not legal advice."
6. **Documents & timeline** — Scroll to show imported evidence and chronology.
7. **Request more information** — Click the button; status updates live.
8. **Run Conflict Check** — Show the simulated conflict entities and status badges; note the
   disclaimer that this doesn't replace the firm's formal procedure.
9. **Schedule Consultation** — Fill the inline form; note it also creates a calendar event.
10. **Open referral `ref-5`** (accepted, ready to convert) — Click "Accept Referral," then "Convert
    to Matter."
11. **Convert to Matter modal** — Fill matter name, responsible lawyer, supporting team, stage;
    submit. You're routed straight into the new matter workspace.
12. **Assign lawyer and team** — Already set during conversion; show the Overview tab's team list.
13. **Documents & Timeline tabs** — Show that referral documents and chronology were imported
    automatically.
14. **Tasks tab** — Create a task with an assignee, due date, and priority.
15. **Communications tab** — Send a secure client message; note the "demo delivery log" disclaimer
    (no real email is sent).
16. **Back to Dashboard** — Show the new matter and updated counts reflected immediately.
17. **Analytics (`/analytics`)** — Walk through acceptance rate, conversion funnel, decline reasons,
    and lawyer workload charts.
18. **Subscription (`/subscription`)** — Show the 5-tier illustrative pricing and upgrade CTA to
    close on the business model.

**Good closing line:** "Everything you just saw — the referral, the score, the documents — started
as a five-minute consumer intake in JusticeChamp. That's the pitch: JusticeChamp fills the top of
the funnel, SolonIQ runs the firm."
